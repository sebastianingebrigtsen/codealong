## Why two extensions?

|                                      | Does                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **CodeAlong for VS Code** (this one) | Notices _that_ you are typing or saving. It never reads or sends your code. |
| **CodeAlong for Chrome**             | Finds the video on the tutorial tab and pauses or plays it.                 |

They talk over a private connection on your own computer (`127.0.0.1`). No account, no cloud, no analytics.

Once both are installed they find each other automatically. There is nothing to configure.
